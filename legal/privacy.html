Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

Training Arc is a workout tracker that turns your training into an RPG. This policy explains what information the app keeps, where it lives, and what you can do about it. The short version: your training stays on your device unless you choose to sign in, we don't run ads or tracking, and we never sell your data.

Without an account

You can use Training Arc without signing up. Everything you enter stays in your browser's or device's storage, on that device only:

  • your profiles, character name and look, rank, level and items;
  • your workouts, sets, maxes, programs, the sport you pick, and progress;
  • Combine Day results (jumps, sprint and agility times, push-ups, mile time) and the ratings made from them;
  • food, water and nutrition entries, foods you create yourself, and any body details you enter for nutrition targets;
  • settings and preferences.

We can't see any of this. Removing a profile in the app, clearing the app's storage, or deleting the app removes it.

With an account (optional)

Signing in lets you back up your progress and add friends. If you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, used only to send you sign-in links and codes. There's no password.
  • Your cloud save: a copy of your profile's progress, so you can restore it or use it on another device.
  • Your player card: the name, character look, stage, rank, level, overall rating, stats, maxes, streaks, medals, trophies and weekly training numbers your friends see.
  • Your friend code, friends and friend requests.
  • Social activity: things you share in the friends feed, reactions, challenges, cheers and messages you send.

Friends you accept can see your player card and the activity you share with them. People who aren't your friends can't. Your email is never shown to other users.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts the account database and sends sign-in emails.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's files. Like any web host, it may briefly log basic request details such as your IP address.

We don't use advertising networks, analytics or tracking tools, and we don't sell or rent your information to anyone. If you tap a "form video" link, it opens a YouTube search in your browser, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.

Health information

Workouts, Combine results, body details and nutrition entries are used only to run the app's features (your plan, targets, ratings and stats). They stay on your device unless you sign in and back up your progress. We don't share them with anyone.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your login, cloud save, player card, friends, requests, feed posts, reactions and messages from our servers.
  • Data on your device: remove the profile in the app, or clear the app's storage, or delete the app.

Children

Training Arc isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
